# Supply Chain Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI supply-chain analytics dashboard** designed to help business and operations leaders monitor revenue, sales volume, logistics efficiency, and product profitability through executive-level KPIs and interactive analysis.

The dashboard transforms transactional and operational data into actionable business insights using **Power Query, DAX, data modeling, and Power BI visualization**.

> **Portfolio Project:** Supply Chain Analytics & Business Intelligence
> **Tools:** Power BI · DAX · Power Query

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Supply-chain teams need a consolidated view of commercial performance and operational efficiency to identify profitable products, monitor logistics performance, and detect areas requiring management attention.

This dashboard provides an interactive analytical layer covering:

* Revenue performance
* Unit sales
* Product profitability
* Logistics efficiency
* Operational KPIs
* Product/category performance
* Management-level business insights

The dashboard is designed from an **executive decision-support perspective**, rather than simply presenting raw transactional data.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard addresses questions such as:

1. How is overall revenue performing?
2. Which products and categories contribute the most revenue?
3. Which products generate the strongest profitability?
4. Where are logistics operations performing efficiently or inefficiently?
5. Which areas require further investigation from management?
6. How can operational and commercial KPIs be monitored from a single dashboard?

## 🧩 Dashboard Components

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of:

* Revenue
* Units sold
* Profitability
* Overall business performance
* Key product/category trends

Designed for leadership-level monitoring and rapid identification of business signals.

### 2. Product & Profitability Analysis

Analyzes product-level performance to identify:

* High-revenue products
* High-profit products
* Low-performing products
* Product/category contribution
* Profitability patterns

This helps distinguish between products that generate significant sales and products that generate significant economic value.

### 3. Logistics Efficiency

Provides visibility into operational performance through logistics-related KPIs and comparisons.

The analysis can be used to identify areas where operational efficiency may require further investigation.

### 4. Interactive Business Analysis

Power BI filters and cross-highlighting allow users to analyze performance across relevant business dimensions and move from high-level KPIs to more detailed operational views.

## ⚠️ Scope & Limitations

This dashboard is intended as a portfolio/business-intelligence project.

The results should be interpreted within the scope of the underlying dataset and its assumptions.

The dashboard provides **descriptive and diagnostic business intelligence**; operational recommendations should be validated against current business processes, data quality, and organizational constraints before implementation.
